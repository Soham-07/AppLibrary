04
THE EMOTIONAL TRAP
The people you follow and the real reason you keep opening the app

This is the section most people skip in self-analysis. Don't.

There are specific people in your social circle whose profiles you check. Not for information. Not for inspiration. For emotional reasons you haven't fully named yet. Your brain didn't land on those profiles randomly — it landed there because those are the real hooks keeping you on the app. Not tutorials. Not 3D art feeds. People you have unresolved feelings about.

The Instagram algorithm is not neutral. It tracks your hesitation, your rewatch patterns, your scroll-stop moments. It knows which profiles make you pause. And it will keep serving those to you because that's what keeps you inside the app. This is not a glitch. This is the product.

Ask yourself honestly: if those specific people didn't have Instagram accounts — would you still open the app 10 times a day? The answer to that question is the real answer to this whole decision.

09
WHAT TO ACTUALLY DO
Specific, ordered, no ambiguity

Don't say "forever." That's why you've failed 1,000 times. "Forever" triggers rebellion. Your brain fights absolutes. Instead — frame this as a 60-day structured break with a defined re-entry condition.

  1. Today — Deactivate the personal account. Not delete. Deactivate. This is reversible, which removes the psychological pressure of permanence. But deactivate it now — not after one more scroll.
  2. Leave the professional account dormant. Don't touch it. Don't scroll from it. Set a locked screen time limit on the app itself at the OS level — not just willpower.
  3. Replace the urge — specifically. When you reach for Instagram, open your Maya project file instead. Not YouTube. Not WhatsApp. The project file. Even if you just stare at it for 3 minutes. Break the motor habit, not just the thought.
  4. On Day 60 — reactivate the professional account only if you have at least one completed piece of work to post. This turns Instagram from a comfort into a reward. If you don't have work yet, extend by 30 days. No exceptions.
  5. Never reactivate the personal account. You don't need it. Everyone who matters can reach you on WhatsApp. The personal account was always the problem — not the professional one.
