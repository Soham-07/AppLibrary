DOT Overview

🚧 What is DOT?

Department of Transportation handles road maintenance, cleanup, and traffic control in Liberty County. You respond to real calls like debris, oil spills, downed trees, potholes, and vehicle towing.

📍 Office Location

Spawn at DOT Office on Cline Street, near the Power Plant and River City Police Department.

🎮 How to Start

  • Spawn as Civilian
  • Click Menu → Team → DOT
  • Type a call sign (e.g. 1-2-3 or BUN) → Confirm
  • Spawn at DOT office on Cline St
  • Open V/MDT to see active calls → Start responding!

🔑 Key Controls

  • E   Interact — use tools on calls
  • V / MDT   View all active calls
  • Radio   Coordinate with other DOTs

TRAINEE DOT WORKER

  • WORKLearn calls: road debris, potholes, oil spills, broken street lamps
  • GEARBroom, traffic cones, warning lights, uniform, flashlight, radio
  • GOALPractice calls, understand DOT workflow, earn Trainee XP
  • TIPJoin calls near DOT office first. Use V/MDT constantly. Assist higher ranks on big scenes

DOT OPERATOR (ALL-ROUND)

  • WORKRespond to all DOT calls in your area — debris, potholes, broken lamps, downed trees
  • GEARPickup truck, broom truck, basic tools
  • GOALMost common DOT role. Handle everyday jobs. Good XP grinder
  • TIPUse a fast car to respond to oldest calls first (more XP)

TOW OPERATOR

  • WORKTow cars from crashes, obstructions, and accident scenes
  • GEARTow truck, wrecker rig, chains
  • GOALSpecialize in obstruction calls. Tow fast = earn XP fast
  • TIPSpawn a fast DOT car first. Join tow calls near you. Work with other DOTs on multi-car scenes

STREET SWEEPER

  • WORKSweep roads using sweeper truck. Clear debris, leaves, and trash from streets
  • GEARStreet sweeper truck, warning lights (NO lights/siren during sweep)
  • GOALKeep roads clean. No police/EMS interaction needed. Chill and consistent
  • TIPGreat for passive XP. Low stress. Good for learning the map

TECHNICIAN / SENIOR OPERATOR

  • WORKHandle big jobs: intersection maintenance, road signs, signal lights, heavy repairs
  • GEARHeavy trucks, ladders, road tools
  • GOALExpert DOT. Coordinate with other DOTs on big scenes. Train beginners
  • TIPJoin calls far from DOT office. Use all tools: traffic lights, road signs, cones. Create complex scenes

SUPERVISOR / SENIOR TECHNICIAN

  • WORKLead DOT operations, assign sectors, coordinate all calls
  • RANKSSenior Technician → Supervisor
  • GOALCommand DOT units. Big-scene control. Ensure all DOTs follow safety procedures
  • TIPUse radio constantly. Full DOT mastery needed. Choose any vehicle or tool freely

Daily Grind Plan
BEGINNER TRAINEE → OPERATOR PATH
01

Round 1 — Learn the Calls

Do 10–15 DOT calls near the DOT office. Debris, oil spills, small trees. Use V/MDT constantly. Focus on understanding call workflow.

02

Round 2 — Tow and Big Clears

Spawn a tow truck. Join crash and obstruction calls. Tow vehicles, clear debris. Combine with other DOTs on big scenes.

03

Round 3 — Senior Scene Work

Join calls far from DOT office. Use all tools: cones, traffic lights, road signs. Create full scene setups. Coordinate via radio.

ADVANCED TECHNICIAN → SUPERVISOR PATH
04

Max XP Strategy

Always join the OLDEST calls first from MDT. Use a fast vehicle to get there first. Join 2–3 DOTs to one big call. Join active DOT servers for bonus XP and more vehicles.

05

Supervisor Grind

Lead big-scene DOT ops. Assign sectors to other DOTs. Radio coordination on complex calls. Full mastery of all tools and vehicles needed.

Pro Tips
📋

Oldest calls = most XP. Always sort MDT by oldest and hit those first before others grab them.

🚗

Speed is XP. Always spawn a fast DOT vehicle so you reach calls before other DOTs.

👥

Stack up on big calls. 2–3 DOTs on one big scene = more XP for everyone. Team up.

🚦

Cones always go first. Set up traffic cones to block roads before working any call. Safety = RP points.

🌐

Join DOT-specific servers. Dedicated DOT servers give extra XP, more vehicles, and active co-workers.

📻

Radio is your best friend. Coordinate constantly. Announce your location and ETA to avoid overlaps.
